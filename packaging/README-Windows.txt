MPC3K Delay - Windows
=====================

A recreation of the Akai MPC3000's built-in 3-tap delay.

INSTALL (about 1 minute)
------------------------
1. Copy the folder "MPC3K Delay.vst3" into:

       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3

   (Windows will ask for admin permission - click Continue.)

2. Open your DAW and rescan plugins. It shows up as "MPC3K Delay".

That's it. Nothing else to install.

JUST WANT TO TRY IT?
--------------------
Double-click "MPC3K Delay.exe" to run it on its own, no DAW needed.
In its Options/Settings, pick your audio interface and turn on an input.

IF WINDOWS SAYS "Windows protected your PC"
--------------------------------------------
This appears for apps from small developers. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

QUICK START
-----------
- Tap 1 starts on. Set its Time to ~375 ms and Feedback to ~30%.
- Turn up Tap 2 and Tap 3 Volume for more echoes at other times.
- Careful: if the three Feedback knobs add up to more than 100%,
  the echoes grow forever (just like the real MPC3000). Keep your volume low.
